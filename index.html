HELP CENTRE

Frequently asked questions

Are these product prices final?

No. They are editable starter estimates in Kenyan shillings. Verify supplier prices, brand-specific refill prices, stock, bottle deposits and any cylinder exchange conditions before accepting live orders.

How is the delivery fee calculated?

The checkout estimates a fee using the delivery zone selected by the customer. You should set zone prices based on your actual rider costs and confirm the fee before dispatch. The admin should verify every unusual or out-of-zone order.

Can customers pay automatically through M-Pesa?

The project includes a Daraja STK Push integration scaffold. You must register and configure the correct Safaricom Daraja app, shortcode, passkey, public HTTPS callback URL and production access before live payments will work. Test using the sandbox first.

How do I contact the business?

Use the WhatsApp order button at checkout. Before launch, set your official Kenyan WhatsApp number in the server environment and add a monitored business phone and email in the footer.

What about gas safety?

Use authorized suppliers and trained delivery personnel. Follow applicable Kenyan LPG safety and licensing requirements. Never deliver damaged or leaking cylinders; arrange installation only through qualified personnel.
